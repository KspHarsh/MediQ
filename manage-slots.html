Create Individual 10-Minute Slot

Every slot must have an exact duration of 10 minutes (e.g. 09:00–09:10, 09:10–09:20). Non-10 minute windows are rejected.

💡 Entering a start time automatically presets the 10-minute end time. You can manually create multiple slots throughout the day for each doctor. Overlapping slots are automatically detected and blocked.
