About MediQ System

Deterministic slot-based outpatient scheduling architecture

Purpose & Operating Principles

MediQ was engineered to solve the unpredictability of outpatient hospital departments (OPD). Unlike generic calendar systems that generate bulk blocks of time, MediQ enforces individual, doctor-specific 10-minute consultation slots manually created by hospital administrative staff.

Strict 10-Minute Duration Invariant: Every consultation slot configured in MediQ has an exact duration of 10 minutes (for example, 09:00–09:10, 09:10–09:20). Slots with durations not equal to 10 minutes (e.g. 09:00–09:15) are strictly rejected by the system.

Workflow & State Invariants

1. Administrative Control

Doctors do not have rigid, automatically assumed working hours. Hospital administrators specify customized schedules per doctor and date, preventing schedule conflicts and ensuring real-time operational flexibility.

2. Collision Prevention

When a patient submits an appointment request, the selected slot is temporarily locked in a PENDING reservation. No other patient can select or double-book that slot during this window.

3. Token Issuance

Upon administrative review and approval, the slot status converts to BOOKED, and an official printable consultation token is generated for the patient, complete with clinical tracking details.

Static Prototype Architecture Notice

This implementation is a first-phase static frontend demonstration built exclusively with HTML5, CSS3, and modern vanilla JavaScript (ES6+). Data is persisted in browser localStorage. As a static prototype without a server-side database:

  • Data is local to this specific browser and origin; records are not synchronized across distinct physical devices or other browsers.
  • Client-side route guards and demo password hashing simulate demonstration authentication workflows only and do not constitute a cryptographic production security boundary.
  • All hospital names, doctors, and appointment identifiers are demonstration placeholders.
